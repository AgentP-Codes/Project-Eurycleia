# Roadmap

Plans, not promises. They change as test results and users come in.

## 0.7.x

- Done in 0.7.1: a person approves refunds, seeing Eurycleia's facts rather than the AI's account.
- Live results from more models: OpenAI and Claude, small and large, with refunds approved.
- Ask a person only when an action is unusual (a bigger amount, a new address), so routine work runs on its own.

## 0.8

New kinds of agents: tools and connectors that can't change behind your back, memory that remembers where
information came from, and approvals that show the facts rather than the agent's summary. Results mapped to the
OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications and MITRE ATLAS. Released in steps.

## 0.9

Agents that use a screen or browser, agents that talk to other agents, outside red-team testing and an
independent security review.

## 1.0

Stable: a policy format and API that won't break without notice, signed releases, and deployment guides.

Every release also keeps up with new agent capabilities and newly published attacks.
